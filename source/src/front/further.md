## Further adventures in Mumbai and beyond

The best biology laboratory is not inside a screen—it is right outside your door in Mumbai:

- **Sanjay Gandhi National Park (Borivali) & Maharashtra Nature Park (Dharavi):** Walk the trail to Kanheri Caves before the June monsoon and again two weeks after the first rain to watch dormant spores, mosses, *Karvi* shrubs, and forest food webs wake up in real time.
- **Thane Creek & Sewri Mudflats (November to March):** Watch tens of thousands of Lesser and Greater Flamingos filter-feeding on blue-green algae (*Spirulina* / cyanobacteria) in the tidal mudflats, bordered by salt-excreting *Avicennia marina* mangroves.
- **Bombay Natural History Society (BNHS), Hornbill House, Colaba:** Visit the institution where ten-year-old Salim Ali brought his yellow-throated sparrow in 1906, and explore their field guides and natural history collections.
- **Chai and Why? (TIFR Mumbai):** Twice a month at Prithvi Theatre in Juhu, Ruparel College in Matunga, or Alexandra Girls' English Institution in Fort, scientists from the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research run hands-on public science demonstrations where you can look through microscopes, extract DNA, and ask any question you like.
