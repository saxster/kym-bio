## Recomputed Numbers (Python Verified)
- **Leeuwenhoek's Utrecht lens:** focal length $f \approx 0.94\text{ mm}$, magnification $M \approx 250 / f \approx 266\times\text{–}275\times$, resolution $\approx 1.35\text{ }\mu\text{m}$.
- **Magnification worked example (0.4):** A *Paramecium* of actual length $A = 200\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 0.20\text{ mm}$ drawn at image length $I = 54\text{ mm}$ has magnification $M = I \div A = 54\text{ mm} \div 0.20\text{ mm} = 270\times$.
- **Dry *moong* bean (*Vigna radiata*) imbibition (1.1):** Dry seed water content $\approx 9\text{–}10\%$ by mass. A $50\text{ g}$ dry sample soaked for 8 hours in Mumbai tap water ($\approx 28^\circ\text{C}$) absorbs $\approx 55\text{ g}$ of water, reaching $\approx 105\text{ g}$ wet mass ($\approx 2.1\times$ initial mass). Respiration rate rises from $<0.05\text{ }\mu\text{L O}_2\text{ g}^{-1}\text{ h}^{-1}$ (dormant glassy cytoplasm) to $>120\text{ }\mu\text{L O}_2\text{ g}^{-1}\text{ h}^{-1}$ within 12 hours of hydration (over $2,000\times$ increase).
- **ATP turnover in the human body (1.3):** Standard free energy of ATP hydrolysis $\Delta G^\circ\prime = -30.5\text{ kJ/mol}$ ($\approx -50\text{ kJ/mol}$ under cellular concentrations); molar mass of ATP $= 507.18\text{ g/mol}$. At a daily metabolic expenditure of $\approx 8,400\text{ kJ}$ ($2,000\text{ kcal}$), with roughly $40\%$ captured as ATP ($\approx 3,360\text{ kJ}$) and each mole yielding $\approx 50\text{ kJ}$, the body turns over $\approx 67\text{ mol/day} \approx 34\text{ kg}$ to $50\text{ kg}$ of ATP per day from a standing pool of only $\approx 50\text{ g}$ (each ATP molecule is recycled $\approx 700\text{–}1,000$ times a day!).
- **Viral vs. bacterial scale (1.5):** Poliovirus / rhinovirus diameter $\approx 30\text{ nm}$; SARS-CoV-2 diameter $\approx 100\text{ nm}$ ($0.1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$); *E. coli* bacterium $\approx 2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ long $\times 1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ wide (volume $\approx 1,000\times$ larger than a $100\text{ nm}$ virus).

## Fidelity Traps to Avoid
1. **Never say ATP has a "high-energy bond" that releases energy simply when broken.** Breaking any covalent bond *absorbs* energy (*The Invisible World* Ch 7). Energy is released because the products of hydrolysis ($\text{ADP} + \text{P}_i$, hydrated in water with relieved negative-charge repulsion on the three phosphate oxygens) sit at a *lower total potential energy* (stronger newly formed O–H bonds with water) than the reactants! Explain the three negative charges (`− − −`) on the triphosphate tail pushing apart like a compressed spring when water hydrolyses the terminal phosphate.
2. **Never say dormant seeds have "zero metabolism" or are "dead and come back to life".** Dry orthodox seeds form a dense intracellular **glass (vitrified cytoplasm)** that slows molecular diffusion to near zero, preserving folded enzymes and mRNA intact for years until water re-plasticises the cytoplasm.
3. **Never confuse egestion with excretion.** Undigested cellulose fibre passing through the gut tunnel never crossed a cell membrane or took part in cellular metabolism.
