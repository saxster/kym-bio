## Step 0 — Editorial Pitch & Hybrid Architecture Contract (`Hybrid` Mode · Part I Linear Foundation)
- **Reader Profile:** Kym (12, Mumbai, kitchen & everyday Mumbai biology anchors, single `:::school` bridge to IB MYP / Cambridge IGCSE 0610).
- **Book Architecture Mode:** `Hybrid` — Part I (Volumes I–II, Chapters 0–6) is a linear *Head First* mental-model staircase (`standsOn` → `unlocks`); Part II (Volumes III–IV, Chapters 7–15) provides modular *For Dummies* reference playbooks with inline 2-sentence `standsOn` bridges.
- **Central Argument (Chapter 1):** Life is not a static checklist of surface traits shared by a candle flame, nor a vital fluid; it is a membrane-bounded cellular system that uses enzyme-coupled free-energy transfers (such as ATP reactions) to maintain internal order and copy inherited DNA instructions.
- **Target Misconception (`buggyRule` vs `coherentWorldRule`):**
  - `buggyRule`: *"Anything that moves, consumes fuel, takes in $\text{O}_2$, and releases $\text{CO}_2$ and heat is alive; dormant dry seeds are dead until water magically revives them; and snapping ATP's terminal bond releases energy by itself."*
  - `coherentWorldRule`: In a *Buggy World* where uncoupled heat release or isolated bond-snapping drove cellular work, a candle flame would build cells and hydrolysing ATP in a beaker beside a motor protein would spin the motor; instead, uncoupled oxidation dissipates $100\%$ of available free energy ($\Delta G^\circ\prime \approx -2870\text{ kJ/mol}$) without banking ATP or driving work.
- **15-Minute Reader Payoff:** Distinguish hydration swelling from metabolic growth, explain why ATP reactions must be physically coupled to cellular machinery, separate excretion from egestion, and classify viruses at the cellular border.
- **Explicit Scope Fence:** Full enzyme kinetics ($V_{\max}, K_m$) wait for Chapter 4; detailed proton-motive chemiosmosis across cristae waits for Chapter 6; full codon translation waits for Chapter 11.
- **Chain Links:** `standsOn: Chapter 0 (Controlled experiments, cell theory & micrometre/nanometre scale)` → `unlocks: Chapter 2 (Compartmentalised 3D cell architecture & surface-area-to-volume limits)`.

## Fidelity Traps to Avoid
1. **ATP coupling:** Bond breaking requires energy. Whole-reaction Gibbs free energy, product stability and hydration, concentration and charge effects matter. Hydrolysis and phosphate transfer to another molecule are distinct pathways. Do not make the compressed-spring analogy a complete mechanism.
2. **Never say dormant seeds have "zero metabolism" or are "dead and come back to life".** Dry desiccation-tolerant seeds form a dense intracellular **glass (vitrified cytoplasm)** that restricts molecular mobility enormously; slow chemical deterioration can still occur (Ballesteros & Walters 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6646689/).
3. **Never confuse egestion with excretion.** Undigested cellulose fibre passing through the gut tunnel never crossed a cell membrane or took part in cellular metabolism.
4. **Reader revision safeguards (1 November 2026):** Keep all reader-revision scientific accuracy constraints intact: chosen hydration mass accounting explicitly labelled as a model state; tadpoles hatch from frog eggs (https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/frogs-a-chorus-of-colors/reproduction/nests-and-nurseries); second law applies to an isolated total system; 1929 ATP identification separated from later bioenergetic coupling (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9903/); viruses may have envelopes and use host ribosomes for proteins while enzymes replicate genomes (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26833/); Viking Mars case compares competing explanations without claiming a single test settled life on Mars (https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking/).
