## Fidelity Traps to Avoid
1. **ATP coupling:** bond breaking requires energy. Whole-reaction Gibbs free energy, product stability and hydration, concentration and charge effects matter. Hydrolysis and phosphate transfer to another molecule are distinct pathways. Do not make the compressed-spring analogy a complete mechanism.
2. **Never say dormant seeds have "zero metabolism" or are "dead and come back to life".** Dry orthodox seeds form a dense intracellular **glass (vitrified cytoplasm)** that slows molecular diffusion to near zero, preserving folded enzymes and mRNA intact for years until water re-plasticises the cytoplasm.
3. **Never confuse egestion with excretion.** Undigested cellulose fibre passing through the gut tunnel never crossed a cell membrane or took part in cellular metabolism.
