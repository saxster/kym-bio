# Chapter 14 grounding — variation, selection and evolutionary evidence

Revised 1 November 2026 after an independent full-manuscript read. This note supersedes the earlier claims that all categorical traits exclude environmental effects, antibiotic-course completion guarantees eradication, water lilies lack wax, or one short protein provides exact divergence dates. Historical records, invented teaching examples and calculations are distinguished below. Earlier gate PASS records describe the earlier build, not approval of this revision.

## Teaching sequence and cross-chapter links

1. Observe numerical variation and separate categories before naming continuous/discontinuous variation. Height often reflects many genes and environment; ABO illustrates inherited categories. These are examples, not universal causal rules.
2. Mutation supplies new DNA variation; meiosis and fertilisation reshuffle inherited variation (Chapters 11–13). Explain the HbS surface substitution and polymerisation rather than asserting that Ramachandran mapped a new HBB fold.
3. Replica plating tests whether resistance can pre-exist exposure. Natural selection concerns inherited differences in contribution to descendants. Differential survival is one route, along with mating success and fertility; mutation, drift and migration also affect populations.
4. Explain dry-habitat, floating and submerged leaves separately. Link humid boundary layers to water-vapour diffusion (Chapters 3/5), and gas spaces to respiration in waterlogged tissues (Chapter 6).
5. Distinguish artificial selection, inbreeding depression and population uniformity. Explain sequence comparison before clock estimation; fossils constrain ages and histories rather than serving as perfect calendars.
6. Return to the sickle-cell question with explicit hypothetical-population assumptions, the Punnett ratio and a calculated recessive-selection trajectory.

## Source-backed scientific and historical claims

### 14.1 Variation and mutation

- Continuous/discontinuous describe phenotypic patterns. A smooth normal distribution is not required. Discrete traits can have complex genetic and environmental causes. Falconer (1965), primary threshold-character analysis: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-1809.1965.tb00500.x . Temperature-dependent sex provides an experimental categorical environmental example: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8187015/ .
- A trained muscle or suntan is not passed as a new gamete DNA allele. Avoid the universal claim that unchanged sequence excludes all inheritance: maternal effects and some inherited epigenetic states are different mechanisms. NHGRI: https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/fact-sheets/Epigenomics-Fact-Sheet . Do not imply established inheritance of a trained human muscle or suntan.
- Mutation is not specified by what would help an organism. Probabilities can vary among sites. Monroe et al. (2022), primary study: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-04269-6 . Some antibiotics increase mutagenesis without directing a particular useful change: Pribis et al. (2019), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30948267/ . This does not overturn pre-existing-variant selection.
- Luria and Delbrück (1943) tested phage resistance, not a modern antibiotic treatment course: https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/28.6.491 . Lederberg and Lederberg (1952) replica plating: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC169282/ . Coordinates C3/C7/C11 and the twelve-colony schematic are invented. Recovery and purification from an unexposed source supports pre-existing resistance; a source colony is not automatically 100% resistant after one pick.
- HbS coding DNA GAG→GTG yields mRNA GAG→GUG and traditional mature-chain Glu6→Val6. Hydrophobic surface contacts promote deoxygenated HbS polymerisation: primary mechanistic study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28465180/ . Genetic/protein nomenclature conventions should not be confused with full precursor numbering.

### 14.2 Selection and stewardship

- Selection acts through relative reproductive contribution. The six-step classroom chain is a useful explanation of the examples, not an obligatory chronology for every case of selection.
- Keep clinical advice to following the prescribing clinician's dose/duration and seeking advice before changes. No universal longer-course advantage is asserted. UKHSA guidance, section Extend: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/antimicrobial-stewardship-start-smart-then-focus/start-smart-then-focus-antimicrobial-stewardship-toolkit-for-inpatient-care-settings . It explains risks of unnecessary prolonged exposure and situations supporting shorter effective courses. CDC GAS guidance: https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html . No resistant-GAS amoxicillin throat-infection case is used in this chapter.
- Kettlewell's studies support differential predation but recapture is not a direct count of every death. The chapter distinguishes experiments and illustrative curves. Majerus's 4,864-moth release study used six seasons 2002–2007; sleeves around branches were removed before dawn. Natural resting observations include trunks. Cook et al. (2012): https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2011.1136 ; accessible original report https://dash.harvard.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/7312037c-b346-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/content .
- Darwin/Gould finch identification and missing locality information contributed to a developing explanation rather than a single instantaneous proof. Wallace's fever scene is explicitly his later recollection, not a witnessed transcript. Joint presentation: 1 July 1858; Origin: November 1859. Avoid claiming Darwin's child's burial occurred on the presentation date.

### 14.3 Adaptive features

- Rolled marram leaves, sheltered stomata and hairs retain humid still air and restrict water-vapour loss; cactus spines, succulent stems and CAM are separate features, not a universal xerophyte package.
- Floating Nymphaea has upper stomata and can have a hydrophobic wax layer: original field study https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31275754/ . Floating/submerged Nymphaea leaves differ: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquabot.2020.103342 . Do not generalise thin submerged barriers into absence of wax on every floating leaf.
- Elodea has simple thin leaves, not finely divided feathery leaves. Flora of New Zealand: https://www.nzflora.info/factsheet/taxon/Elodea-canadensis.html . Submerged leaves exchange dissolved gases without stomata. Roots remain important for anchorage/nutrients in rooted aquatic plants.
- Leaf measurements are not universal species constants. An illustrative 9.8→1.4 water-loss comparison gives (9.8−1.4)/9.8 = 85.714%, not 92%. No measured dose response or field budget is claimed.
- Camel hump fat is an energy store; its distribution affects insulation. Net heat exchange depends on environmental conditions. Acclimatisation within a lifetime differs from inherited population adaptation; avoid reducing Tibetan/Andean oxygen physiology to haemoglobin coding alleles alone.

### 14.4 Breeding, fossils and sequences

- Janaki Ammal's Coimbatore cytogenetics and hybrid research are retained, without a personal 8.2→16.4% national sucrose-doubling claim. Primary paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/141925a0 ; institutional history: https://www.rhs.org.uk/education-learning/libraries-at-rhs/articles/janaki-ammal . Breeding trajectories are invented, not her cultivar measurements.
- Sahni's original 1948 Pentoxyleae paper called the material Jurassic: https://doi.org/10.1086/335517 . BSIP later identifies relevant Rajmahal specimens as Early Cretaceous: https://www.bsip.res.in/storage/annual_reports/english/AR1991-92.pdf . Mixed anatomy does not by itself establish direct ancestry to modern groups. Cambrian ecosystems contained complex animals, not only simple life.
- Woese and Fox's archaeal discovery dates to 1977; formal three-domain proposal by Woese, Kandler and Wheelis dates to 1990: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2112744/ . Small-subunit rRNA is 16S in bacteria/archaea, 18S in eukaryotic cytoplasm.
- Homologous sequence similarity supports relationship but does not prove a precise root/date. Rates vary among genes and lineages; fossils and other evidence calibrate clocks. Drummond et al. (2006): https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0040088 . No forced linear cytochrome-c/date dataset is presented.
- The familiar human/chimpanzee approximately 1.2% is a single-base difference measure in aligned genomic sequence, not universal coding-DNA identity: original genome paper https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04072 . Identical proteins can be encoded by different codons; this does not explain all genome-wide differences.

## Sickle-cell synthesis: measurements versus model

Allison's historical sample: 113/247 HbAA children had detected parasites (45.7%); 12/43 HbAS (27.9%). Parasitaemia, severe illness and reproductive fitness are different endpoints. Original: https://www.bmj.com/content/1/4857/290 . Later cohort evidence distinguishes asymptomatic carriage and severe malaria: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=%22Sickle+cell+trait+and+the+risk+of+Plasmodium+falciparum+malaria+and+other+childhood+diseases%22 . Mechanisms contributing to protection are not reduced to one definitive explanation.

HbAA and HbSS do not mean literally 100% HbA/HbS; HbA2 and HbF can also occur, varying with age and treatment. Trait is approximately 60:40 HbA:HbS. ASH: https://www.hematology.org/trainees/hematopoiesis/hematopoiesis-articles/2021/hemoglobin-electrophoresis-in-sickle-cell-disease . No numerical fitness values describe modern patients universally.

Populations H/M are hypothetical. Assume large random-mating populations, constant relative reproductive contributions, no migration or mutation. In M, wAA=.85, wAS=1, wSS=.20. With s=.15 and t=.80, q*=s/(s+t)=.1578947368, newborn 2pq=.265927978. After immediate malaria removal set wAA=wAS=1, retain wSS=.20. Recurrence q'=(pq+.20q²)/(p²+2pq+.20q²). Starting q=.158, after20 generations q=.04536554025736232; newborn carriers=.08661501602903993. Decline slows because alleles remain in heterozygotes. A gradual eradication campaign would require a specified changing selection coefficient; it is not this calculation.

## Interactive-model contract

- Variation: height/body-mass distributions and nutrition offsets are invented. ABO/pea category invariance applies to those example loci, not every categorical trait. Rate factors 1/5/20/50 and mutation-effect fractions are illustrative, not agent dose measurements.
- Culture: start S=990, R=10, capacity1000. Per exposed step with pressure d∈[0,1], S←S(1−.9d), R←R(1−.1d). Then space=max(0,1−(S+R)/1000), S←S(1+space), R←R(1+.9space). Variant R therefore has a small reproductive cost. Compare continuous exposure with removal after step3. Numbers are expected values, steps are not days, and there is no immune system or cure/relapse forecast. Controls must affect calculation, not choose fabricated clinical tables.
- Moths: constructed morph-frequency curves illustrate direction, not measured historical timelines or dominant-allele counts.
- HbS: use the stated diploid recurrence each generation. Pressure choices give AA disadvantages0/.08/.15/.20 and SS disadvantage.80. Newborn genotype fractions use random mating before selection. Test boundaries and equilibrium, not only a visually desired curve.
- Plants/breeding: dimensions, performance and trajectories are invented scenarios. Floating leaf upper wax and submerged thin barriers must remain distinct in text and controls.
- Sequences: five synthetic200-base alignments have7/18/36/43/60 mismatches from reference X. Calculate displayed identity from actual strings. Protein differences are also synthetic. No named measured taxon, coding-genome percentage or divergence date is inferred.
- Atlas: Petri-dish selection uses S=990(1−.9d), R=10(1−.1d) before calculating the resistant fraction. Fitness surfaces are illustrative, not a measured universal landscape. HbS SS disadvantage.80 agrees with the prose.

## Home investigation and reader gate

Use dry paper, blunt tweezers and no live organisms/chemicals/heat. Prepare spare dots. Remove exactly30 of60 each round, double survivors and scatter before repeating. Record actual counts and timing, repeat independent trials. Paper colours model asexually copied morphs; they do not directly measure diploid moth alleles. A lost colour cannot reappear merely by changing background. Worked example8white/22printed survivors becomes16/44, or73.3% printed; no promised73→88→95% outcome.

Four Understand-it cards use the five required core questions, three folded questions and a one-sentence learner prompt. Exam detail remains in school/check boxes. Explain-back: Individuals differ, and mutations add inherited variation without knowing what will help. A variant can increase when its carriers leave more offspring in their surroundings. Heterozygote advantage can maintain HbS where malaria changes reproductive success, even though two copies can cause severe disease.
