- **Target Misconception (`buggyRule` vs `coherentWorldRule`):**
  - `buggyRule`: *"Maggots and microbes form spontaneously out of rotting meat, flour, or warm broth; boiling broth ruins the air needed to generate life; and molecules, viruses, bacteria, and cells are all 'microscopic specks' of roughly the same size."*
  - `coherentWorldRule`: In a *Buggy World* where non-living broth spontaneously generated bacteria (`N₀ = 0 ⟹ N(t) > 0`), Pasteur's open-tipped swan-neck flask would turn cloudy without dust contact; in physical reality, Stokes settling ($v_s = 2(\rho_p - \rho_f)gr^2 / 9\eta$) traps airborne parent spores in the lower U-bend while fresh air diffuses freely, and biological structures span six orders of magnitude from $0.28\text{ nm}$ water molecules to $100\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ plant cells.
- **15-Minute Reader Payoff:** Identify independent, dependent, and controlled variables in Redi's and Pasteur's experiments, explain how single-ball-lens microscopes resolve micron-scale bacteria, convert $\text{mm} \leftrightarrow \mu\text{m} \leftrightarrow \text{nm}$, and calculate unitless magnification $M = I \div A$.
- **Explicit Scope Fence:** Thermodynamic coupling and ATP wait for Chapter 1; internal eukaryotic organelle ultrastructure waits for Chapter 2.
- **Chain Links:** `standsOn: Everyday observation & basic arithmetic` → `unlocks: Chapter 1 (Coupled ATP bioenergetics, seed dormancy & what makes a system alive)`.

## Recomputed Numbers (`model.js` + `checks.mjs` Verified)
- **Leeuwenhoek's Utrecht lens (`ch00`):** For a soda-lime glass sphere ($n = 1.52$) of radius $r = 0.75\text{ mm}$, effective focal length $f = \frac{n r}{2(n - 1)} \approx 1.096\text{ mm}$ and magnification $M = 250 / f \approx 228\times$; for $f \approx 0.94\text{ mm}$ (Utrecht surviving lens), $M \approx 266\times\text{–}275\times$, resolution $\approx 1.35\text{ }\mu\text{m}$.
- **Magnification worked example (0.4):** A *Paramecium* of actual length $A = 200\text{ }\mu\text{m} = 0.20\text{ mm}$ drawn at image length $I = 54\text{ mm}$ has magnification $M = I \div A = 54,000\text{ }\mu\text{m} \div 200\text{ }\mu\text{m} = \times 270$. Chloroplast check: $I = 36\text{ mm} = 36,000\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ at $\times 6,000 \implies A = 6\text{ }\mu\text{m}$.
- **Chosen hydration mass-accounting model (1.1):** Holding $45\text{ g}$ non-water dry material fixed: at $10\%$ water fraction, total mass $= 45 / (1 - 0.10) = 50\text{ g}$ ($5\text{ g}$ water); at $65\%$ water fraction, total mass $= 45 / (1 - 0.65) = 128.57\text{ g}$ ($128.6\text{ g}$). If $50\text{ g}$ dry seeds ($45\text{ g}$ dry solids $+ 5\text{ g}$ water) absorb $55\text{ g}$ water, total wet mass becomes $105\text{ g}$, containing $60\text{ g}$ water ($57.14\% \approx 57.1\%$ water).
- **Coupled oxidation free-energy budget (1.2):** Standard transformed $\Delta G^\circ\prime$ of glucose oxidation $\approx 2870\text{ kJ/mol}$; banking $32\text{ ATP}$ at $30.5\text{ kJ/mol}$ captures $976\text{ kJ/mol}$ ($34.0\%$ share), leaving $1894\text{ kJ/mol}$ unassigned free energy. In the *Buggy World* uncoupled state, $0\text{ ATP}$ is banked ($0\text{ kJ/mol}$, $0\%$) and $2870\text{ kJ/mol}$ dissipates uncoupled.
- **Illustrative ATP turnover (1.3):** Assumed adult-scale pool $50\text{ g}$ and daily turnover $40,000\text{ g}$ give $40,000 \div 50 = 800\text{ pool-equivalents/day}$ and $1,440 \div 800 = 1.8\text{ minutes/equivalent}$ (illustrative average, not a uniform individual molecule schedule).
- **Viral vs. bacterial scale & Caspar–Klug geometry (1.5):** For $(h, k) = (2, 1)$, triangulation number $T = h^2 + hk + k^2 = 7$, giving $10(T - 1) + 12 = 72\text{ capsomeres}$ and $60T = 420\text{ subunits}$. SARS-CoV-2 diameter $\approx 100\text{ nm}$ ($0.1\text{ }\mu\text{m}$); *E. coli* bacterium $\approx 2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$ long.

## Fidelity Traps to Avoid
1. **ATP coupling:** Bond breaking requires energy. Whole-reaction Gibbs free energy, product stability and hydration, concentration and charge effects matter. Hydrolysis and phosphate transfer to another molecule are distinct pathways. Do not make the compressed-spring analogy a complete mechanism.
2. **Never say dormant seeds have "zero metabolism" or are "dead and come back to life".** Dry desiccation-tolerant seeds form a dense intracellular **glass (vitrified cytoplasm)** that restricts molecular mobility enormously; slow chemical deterioration can still occur (Ballesteros & Walters 2019, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6646689/).
3. **Never confuse egestion with excretion.** Undigested cellulose fibre passing through the gut tunnel never crossed a cell membrane or took part in cellular metabolism.
4. **Reader revision safeguards (1 November 2026):** Keep all reader-revision scientific accuracy constraints intact: chosen hydration mass accounting explicitly labelled as a model state; tadpoles hatch from frog eggs (https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/frogs-a-chorus-of-colors/reproduction/nests-and-nurseries); second law applies to an isolated total system; 1929 ATP identification separated from later bioenergetic coupling (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9903/); viruses may have envelopes and use host ribosomes for proteins while enzymes replicate genomes (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK26833/); Viking Mars case compares competing explanations without claiming a single test settled life on Mars (https://science.nasa.gov/mission/viking/).
