## Section maps, causal sequence and fidelity traps

### 7.1 — A tube and a barrier

Map: OS34.1 human oral cavity→rectum; OS34.3 ingestion/absorption/elimination; NIDDK organs/uses; Cambridge7.2. Start buttered pav starch and fat: chewing removes chunks, not all molecular covalent links. Ingestion brings food/drink into canal; physical digestion divides food without changing molecules; chemical digestion makes different molecules; absorption crosses epithelial barrier; assimilation is uptake/use by cells; egestion removes unabsorbed/undigested remnants as faeces. Excretion removes metabolic waste or excess substances: faeces also contain microbes, shed cells and excreted substances, so “nothing in faeces ever entered the body” is false. Lumen is continuous with outside through mouth/anus, physically enclosed by body outline but separated from tissues. Not a sealed empty tunnel or an assertion gut contents have no bodily effects. Accessory organs secrete **into** canal, not food waystations. Nutrition supplies material for growth/repair and chemical fuel for metabolism; digestion≠respiration, no energy creation or direct digestion ATP yield. Water, ions, glucose and many vitamins can be absorbed without prior covalent digestion; membranes depend on chemistry/transporters as well as size. Original route and barrier plates.

### 7.2 — Motion, chemistry, protection

Map: NIDDK movement, OS34.1/34.3, MIT4 chemistry, B1833 First Series, AP23.4. Incisors cut, canines tear, premolars/molars crush/grind. Brief tooth cross-section identifies enamel, dentine, pulp vessels/nerves, root cement and bone/gum anchoring; no claim human canines imply meat requirement. Coordinated wall contraction behind/relaxation ahead propels; local contractions mix, stomach churns. Never propose inverted eating or swallowing experiment. Salivary amylase starts starch→maltose/shorter chains; pancreatic amylase continues, brush-border maltase etc produce absorbable glucose. Pepsin starts protein→peptides under acid conditions; pancreatic trypsin/other proteases and epithelial peptidases continue. Protein digestion is not complete in stomach; some small peptides enter enterocytes then hydrolyse. HCl supports pepsin activation/function, protein unfolding and killing many microbes; neither enzyme nor perfect steriliser. pH varies with meals, place/time. Hydrolysis uses water to change covalent connections; enzymes lower barrier/recycle, do not live/die or provide net fuel. Mucus holds bicarbonate near surface, junctions restrict leakage, repair/replacement maintains barrier; pepsinogen limits premature protease activity. Pancreatic proteases also secreted inactive/activated in intestine (trypsinogen→trypsin), rather than every enzyme inactive everywhere. Protection can fail, ulcers have multiple causes; no single universal cause. Original movement/tooth, compartment-chemistry and protection plates. One relevant story, beyond chapter summary: observation/inference and unequal research relationship.

### 7.3 — Two different changes in fat

Map: OS34.3 lipid paragraphs, AP23.6, IH09, MIT4 hydrolysis mechanism. Bile made in liver, stored/concentrated gallbladder, delivered into duodenum. Mixing supplies breakup; bile components with water-compatible and fat-compatible regions stabilise dispersed droplets. **Emulsification changes arrangement/interface, not molecular identity**. Lipase acts at oil/water interface with colipase assistance, catalyses ester hydrolysis with water; main pancreatic products2-MAG+two fatty acids. Cambridge fatty acids/glycerol is a net/full-hydrolysis convention, not false claim all pancreatic products are free glycerol. Distinguish droplet (many triglycerides), product molecules, micelle (bile salt/product assembly). Pancreatic bicarbonate is a major neutralising input alongside bile/duodenal secretions; stomach's acid conditions are not every gut enzyme's optimum. Geometry alone cannot measure digestion-rate factor; interfacial chemistry, enzyme supply/mixing also matter. No unsupported1,000× rate/area. Paper geometry activity with clay/dough is optional, no detergent or ingestion. Two plates: equal-sphere geometry and molecular hydrolysis.

### 7.4 — Cross, then carry

Map: OS34.1 villi/portal; OS34.3 lipids/water; AP23.7; KG16; IH09; HF14; NIDDK-GB. Folds are large mucosal folds; villi are tissue projections lined with cells; microvilli are membrane projections of individual absorptive cells. Thin absorptive **epithelium**, not entire wall, may be one cell thick. Extra area helps if transport machinery/supply function; thickness/gradient also matter (Chapter2/3). Glucose sodium cotransporter uses downhill sodium electrochemical movement to drive glucose entry, even uphill glucose; ATPase on tissue-facing membrane restores ion difference. Separate lumen→cell from cell→tissue fluid→capillary and bulk portal transport to liver→general circulation. Amino acids use transporters; some small peptides enter before final hydrolysis. Fructose demonstrates facilitated diffusion, not all active. Long-chain fat products leave micelles at surface, enter epithelium, rebuild triglycerides, combine with protein/other lipids as chylomicrons, exit into tissue fluid/lacteals, lymph→blood near heart. Intact emulsion droplets/micelles do not travel unchanged into lacteals. Some shorter-chain fatty acids use portal blood; do not generalise all fat-derived substances. Most water absorption small intestine under ordinary conditions, colon recovers more; water also secreted into canal. Assimilation uses absorbed amino acids to build proteins, glucose as fuel or stored glycogen, lipids for membranes/storage; not absorption renamed. Adult surface estimate as evidence with limits. End fictional gallbladder case then delivery/gas-exchange problem for Chapter8. Nested-villus and transport-route plates.
