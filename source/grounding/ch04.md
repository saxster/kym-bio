## 4.1 Water's sticky geometry and carbon's four hands

Teaching sequence: familiar rice/dal/oil → same few elements, different connections → water's uneven electron distribution and bent geometry → partial charges (whole molecule neutral), hydrogen bonds BETWEEN molecules versus covalent O–H INSIDE → dissolving and surrounding environment → carbon commonly makes four covalent bonds, counting a double as two (bonding capacity, no hands/desires) → connected small units, not interchangeable Lego.

Glucose C₆H₁₂O₆ is a small sugar, mostly rings in water. Starch includes unbranched amylose and branched amylopectin, plant storage; glycogen is more highly branched storage in animals, including liver/muscle; cellulose uses different glucose link orientations and forms strong wall fibres. Human digestive enzymes break starch but lack cellulase; same units do not imply same properties. Monomer/polymer = one/many parts (Merriam-Webster https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/polymer). Assembly changes chemical bonds and can require coupled energy; no child snapping atoms by hand.

Amino acids share amino/carboxyl groups and differ in side chains; commonly 20 types, not only 20 possible biological amino acids. Peptide bonds link an ordered chain; proteins can contain one or more chains. CHON, often S, versus CHO carbohydrates/fats; other elements may occur in conjugated proteins. A triglyceride (fat/oil example) has one glycerol joined to three fatty acids, three ester links; this is NOT an ordinary long repeating-unit polymer, nor a diagram of every lipid. Membrane phospholipids and steroids differ. Saturated/unsaturated chain packing can affect melting, no blanket plant/animal rule.

Evidence: purified carbohydrate structure and digestion comparison (O3.2); plate identifies connections rather than claiming a kitchen assay sees atoms. Home: two small clear lidded containers, cool water, a little cooking oil in one; gentle inversion then settling compared with water-only control. Qualitative phase separation, not exact timing. No detergent ingestion, no heating/glass cutting. Do not taste; wipe oil, absorb small oil waste into household waste; do not pour oil down drain. Paper folding model optional second home: pre-cut strip, no child knife, labelled physical analogy rather than experiment on proteins.

Plates: c4_water_carbon (polarity/bond key; methane connectivity projection explicitly not a flat molecular geometry); c4_building_units (glucose-derived architectures plus ordered amino acids and triglyceride). Chosen token counts are illustration counts, not chain lengths. Card central concepts: water polarity/hydrogen bonding; monomers/polymers; fats/oils. Glucose and amino acids taught within these and protein card next section.

## 4.3 The lock that bends the stick

Start with Chapter 3 selective route: matching outline tempting, chemistry needed. Enzyme active site brings selected reactants close in useful orientation and provides chemical groups that stabilise difficult arrangements/transition states. Binding can change both partners (induced fit); not every enzyme literally bends substrate like a wooden stick. Substrate = reactant acted on; enzyme–substrate complex = temporarily associated molecules, not a new living organism. Products leave and catalytic enzyme is regenerated. Most enzymes proteins; catalytic RNAs (ribozymes) are an accurate short qualification (I04/7.016 recitation3 summary explicitly says so). Specificity may be narrow or a family of related substrates; not an inflexible silhouette lock.

Evidence TRUE: Buchner 1897, original translated preliminary communication https://web.lemoyne.edu/~giunta/buchner.html (Berichte 30:117). Clear yeast press juice, filtration retaining cells, sugar still converted; glucose/fructose/maltose versus lactose/mannitol differences. Does not establish a single isolated purified enzyme or prove all enzymes' mechanism. It showed cell-free chemistry; current interpretation multi-enzyme system. Attribution only, no old unsafe protocols reenacted. Optional narrative use no new cast story required.

Activation barrier = hurdle to reaching reactive arrangement. An enzyme gives lower-barrier route; it supplies neither net energy nor different equilibrium position. Forward AND reverse are accelerated; coupled energy from other reactions needed for otherwise unfavourable overall synthesis. Free energy graph horizontal axis reaction progress, NOT elapsed seconds or physical distance. ΔG unchanged. Bond breaking requires input; overall making/rearranging bonds can release more. Link Book1/Book2.

Title 'million-speed' is a nickname, NOT universal measured multiplier. No numerical real rate enhancement claimed. Widget c4_barrier uses deliberately chosen free-energy units (arbitrary), start20, products10, uncatalysed peak90, catalysed peak55. Forward barriers70/35; reverse80/45; endpoint difference−10, both lowered35. No seconds, measured rates or implied factor from barrier ratio. A stage slider0–4 shows association, interaction, rearrangement, release/reuse; matched chemistry versus wrong group selector. No automatic motion/play controls. Card enzyme/action and activation energy separated.

## 4.4 Why enzymes do not 'die' when heated

I04 pp1–2 and O6.5: warming increases molecular kinetic energy and frequency of effective encounters while enzyme functional; prolonged/strong heat can disrupt stabilising interactions, alter active site and promote aggregation. Denaturation changes functional three-dimensional structure; does not require cutting peptide chain or death. Some denaturation reversible; cooling does not guarantee refolding. pH changes side-chain protonation/charges, affecting binding/catalysis before or without unfolding; extremes may denature. Each enzyme and assay has own optimum; exposure time, substrate and conditions matter. Cold can slow a reaction without denaturation. No universal42°C threshold, no medical advice.

Evidence: compare initial rates of same enzyme/substrate across temperatures and exposure, then cool heated enzyme and compare with fresh enzyme at SAME assay temperature/pH. Residual activity tests persistent loss rather than hotter reactions alone. This is a proposed school comparison (not a historical dataset/physically tested here); no fabricated numerical results. MIT lecture's reversible versus aggregation discussion supplies scientific account. No peroxide home experiment; 6% pharmacy material and liver dissection rejected as unnecessary risk/variable performance. No claim 'zero bubbles' proves no catalase.

c4_conditions: schematic temperature curve (not measured), separate encounter factor and remaining-active fraction; pH panel charge/fit. Widget model at fixed unspecified exposure: E(T)=2^((T−20)/20), F(T)=1/(1+exp((T−50)/4)), H(pH)=exp(−((pH−7)/2)^2), R=E×F×H. Parameters20°C,50°C,4°C, pH7,width2 are INVENTED for teaching, not any named enzyme or human physiology. 'Intact/heat-exposed' comparison uses chosen surviving fraction0.15; switching back is a NEW sample, not spontaneous recovery. pH H is an activity factor, not a denatured fraction. Show units as relative model units, temperature real °C input. Numerically find model optimum; do not label50°C as activity optimum merely because F midpoint there. Card denaturation and optimum.
