## Section-by-section sequence, traps and implementation decisions

### 5.1 The five-year willow tree
A Mumbai shade tree, no invented age/mass → tiny seed cannot supply adult material → test eating soil by weighed inputs/outputs → H ledger → soil loss too small to account for tree gain on the reported figures, but no complete dry organic mass account → water material and CO₂ carbon, soil essential ions. Fresh mass includes water, dry mass after removing water by a specified drying method; original tree not established dry. Fallen leaves missing, measurement uncertainty unknown. Mineral nitrate for amino acids (nitrogen) and magnesium for chlorophyll, C6.1.6; chlorophyll explanation deferred5.3. Card: plant mass. Plate `c5_ledger` separates recorded quantities from missing inputs and inference. At first delivery the older p-helmont story was retained, and its inaccurate account was not linked. The user subsequently authorised its correction; the corrected story and normal Chapter 5 term link now share this qualified account.

### 5.2 Bubbles in the sunlight
Leaves apparently “improve air” → compare changed versus unchanged conditions → P actual separate exposures, I light/green comparisons, S carbon/water accounting → gas evidence not direct molecular observation. Net oxygen = gross oxygenic production minus oxygen-consuming processes (in teaching model only respiration; photorespiration can also matter). Plants respire in light and darkness. Bubble count imperfect: size, dissolution, lag, leaks and temperature; no exact rate/efficiency. School-only aquatic investigation and indicator controls; no live animals or flames. Card: net gas exchange. Plate `c5_gas_evidence`, not an invented single historical experiment combining all three. New story I only; no extra cast inflation.

### 5.3 Inside the green solar panel
Plant cell → chloroplast → thylakoid membranes/pigment light absorption → excited electron transfer; water supplies electrons and released O₂ → temporary ATP energy transfer/NADPH electron carrier → enzymes in stroma incorporate CO₂ carbon into small three-carbon products → larger carbohydrates. School glucose summary; light energy never atoms; enzyme lowers barriers not energy source. Net 6CO₂+6H₂O→C₆H₁₂O₆+6O₂; fuller6CO₂+12H₂O→C₆H₁₂O₆+6O₂+6H₂O. Both balance; cancellation conceals gross water input/output, not literal atom path. Carbon6/12H/18O net; carbon6/24H/24O fuller. Formal mass with C12 H1 O16: net264+108=180+192=372; fuller264+216=180+192+108=480. These are relative formula/mole accounts, not plant yields. Isotope evidence distinguishes O source; do not colour every product oxygen from CO₂ based on this equation.
Uses: starch store, cellulose walls, sucrose transport, respiration, nectar attraction; amino acid production also needs nitrogen. Limiting factors light, CO₂, temperature interact; no universal optimum or endless increase. Proposed controlled school tests light/dark, variegated green/non-green region and CO₂ control with matched healthy material, temperature/water/history/repeats; iodine starch endpoint measures accumulation not immediate rate, hot ethanol school-only no flame. Home no reagents.
Cards: photosynthesis; limiting factors. Plates `c5_chloroplast`, `c5_limits`. Widgets `carbon_account` (manual batch/formal account/stages) and `limiting_lab` (chosen capacities, capture trials; run required for challenge).

### 5.4 Engineering a flat solar factory
Representative dicot (not every leaf) → thin broad blade light capture/short diffusion → cuticle water loss, transparent epidermis, palisade many chloroplasts, spongy mesophyll wet walls/air spaces, vein xylem/phloem → stomata pores; guard cell ion/solute changes alter water potential, water movement/turgor and uneven walls/microfibrils bend cell/open pore, reversal closes. Signals not decisions; some stomata upper/both surfaces, schedules vary, drought closure restricts CO₂ as well as water loss. Not every plant cell chloroplast-bearing. Card leaf structure/stomata. Plate `c5_leaf` geometric cross-section with enlarged surface-view guard-cell inset clearly separate, no invented micrometre scale. Home inspect attached familiar houseplant leaf in place, both surfaces/veins, no tearing/sharp tools; cannot see stomata with unaided eye; no claim physical trial.

### 5.5 Pulling water upwards without a heart
Moist cell walls evaporate → vapour diffuses via stomata into drier air → receding curved air/water interfaces surface tension pulls remaining liquid → lower liquid pressure transmitted via cohesion H-bonds down continuous xylem network → water follows total water-potential gradient soil/root cortex/xylem/mesophyll/air, osmosis specifically across membranes. Vessel elements mature dead/hollow, with lignified walls; perforated end walls connect elements, pits connect neighbouring routes; support under tension. Not open straw atmospheric push or ATP pumps. Cavitation can interrupt, root pressure sometimes contributes not tall-tree general account. Wind removes humid boundary layer; higher humidity reduces vapour gradient; temperature affects evaporation, severe deficit closure/wilting complicates.
Phloem living sieve elements/companion cells, sucrose+amino acids source→sink can up or down in different tubes. Example active loading: ATP-driven H+ pump and cotransport already taught Ch3, sugar accumulation lowers water potential, water enters/osmotic pressure rises, pressure difference drives bulk flow; unloading/use/storage sustains difference and water may rejoin xylem. Some loading passive. Sink growing/storage versus source release; storage organ can switch roles. Cards: transpiration/cohesion–tension; translocation. Plates `c5_xylem` and `c5_phloem`, widget `sap_routes` manual route/stage.
Case: explicitly fictional sealed terrarium in suitable indirect light. Energy crosses, matter nearly closed, water condensation/root uptake, carbon fixation/respiration, plants+decomposers respire continuously; light input needed, heat leaves. No guarantee indefinite life or maintenance-free nutrients; no opening/sniffing mould. Bridge Chapter6 how respiration transfers chemical energy to ATP.

## Model definitions BEFORE implementation

1. carbon_account: integer batches n1–4 of the fuller formal glucose summary. Inputs n×(6CO₂+12H₂O); outputs n×(glucose+6O₂+6H₂O). Five manual stages0–4 focus absorption/water/CO₂/small products/summary; labels disclose that changing focus doesn't run kinetics. One displayed batch is a stoichiometric accounting unit, not one actual glucose step. Count arrays [6n,24n,24n] each side; formal masses480n. Water oxygen→releasedO₂ is isotope-supported; other oxygen paths not assigned. Photon count/efficiency not inferred.
2. limiting_lab: L,C chosen capacity units0–100, T5–45°C; thermal capacity K(T)=max(0,100−((T−25)/2)^2); gross R=min(L,C,K), chosen respiratory oxygen-use baseline10; net=R−10. Deliberately crude bottleneck model, not a fitted law, no real CO₂ ppm, photon flux, bubbles, ml/s or plant optimum. Each slider setting a new independent prepared sample at unspecified common exposure, NO damage/recovery trajectory. Capture stores {L,C,T,R,net}; compare two recent runs; invalid if !=1 factor changed, not inferred merely by sliders. Challenge run trial at20/80/25 then60/80/25; light alone changes, gross20→60, net10→50. Atmospheric oxygen provides respiration when net negative; model no finite jar depletion. Do not equate gross with net.
3. sap_routes: route0 xylem;1 mature leaf→root phloem;2 storage organ→shoot phloem. stage0–4 changes focus on causal chain only, not seconds/displacement/speed. No numerical flow/damage history. Five stages label leaf evaporation/pressure/cohesion/root supply/whole route; phloem loading/osmotic entry/pressure/unload/complete pathway. Arrows always explicitly assigned contents, upward flow for route2, separate drawing per tube direction.
