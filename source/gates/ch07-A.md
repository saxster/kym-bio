## Chapter 7: scientific findings and fidelity

**7.1 — pass.** The chapter separates position in the canal from crossing the epithelium, and distinguishes ingestion, physical/chemical digestion, absorption, assimilation, egestion and excretion. The faeces exception avoids the false claim that every faecal component stayed outside tissues. Some nutrients require no prior covalent digestion. Nutrition supplies matter and chemical fuel; digestion is not respiration or energy creation. The route deliberately omits a pharynx label for the syllabus-level connection map without placing accessory organs in the food path.

**Anatomical connection check — pass.** `c7_canal` connects liver to the shared bile route, gallbladder to its branch in both storage/release directions, and common bile route to duodenum. Its pancreatic route enters the duodenum, not the stomach, gallbladder or downstream colon. Saliva enters the mouth. Food follows its separate solid route. The diagram explicitly disclaims anatomical positions and scale. The gallbladder can be bypassed. Checked against [A&P §23.6](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-6-accessory-organs-in-digestion-the-liver-pancreas-and-gallbladder).

**7.2 — pass subject to A1.** Amylase produces maltose/shorter chains, then brush-border enzymes supply glucose; pepsin does not finish all protein digestion. Water participates in hydrolysis and the complete reaction determines energy transfer. The enzyme changes the activation barrier, not the reactant/product free-energy difference. Stomach pH is variable, not universally 2. Acid supports pepsin and protein unfolding, but is neither pepsin nor a guaranteed steriliser. Mucus/bicarbonate, joins, repair and pepsinogen jointly reduce self-damage; protection can fail. Trypsin precursor activation and small-peptide uptake before intracellular hydrolysis are appropriately qualified. Checked against [A&P §23.4](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-4-the-stomach), [Kiela & Ghishan 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4956471/) and the locally cached [Imperiali Lecture 4 transcript](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-016-introductory-biology-fall-2018/f531ebe6a60d74b7f6b7c10732a4f130_QTdJiG7mV40.pdf), relevant hydrolysis/barrier/catalysis passages. MIT is credited for chemistry, not human digestive anatomy or history.

**7.3 — pass.** The chapter, static plates, widget and appended enhancement separate emulsification from hydrolysis; droplets contain many molecules. Mixing and amphipathic bile components help establish/stabilise dispersion. Lipase acts at an interface with colipase assistance, and the main pancreatic step gives monoacylglycerol plus two fatty acids. The school glycerol summary is labelled as a full/net-breakdown convention. Water-inclusive molecular accounts are balanced. Area is never converted into a measured digestion-rate factor. Pancreatic bicarbonate is correctly identified as a major neutralising input. Checked against [Iqbal & Hussain 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2692399/), especially overview, TAG hydrolysis and resynthesis; formula verification below is independent arithmetic, not a copied reaction figure.

**7.4 — pass.** Villi are tissue projections and microvilli are individual-cell membrane projections; the epithelium rather than the entire wall is one cell thick. Glucose entry uses sodium's electrochemical gradient; the tissue-facing ATPase maintains it. Cell exit, tissue fluid, capillary entry and portal blood flow are separate events. Fructose and peptide contrasts prevent a universal absorption mechanism. Long-chain products leave micelles, enter cells, are rebuilt and packaged into chylomicrons, then enter tissue fluid/lacteals/lymph/blood. Neither whole micelles nor original droplets are delivered unchanged. Some shorter-chain fatty acids use portal blood; this exception does not assert that intact medium-chain triglycerides skip digestion. Both intestines absorb water, usually mainly the small intestine. The [transport review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4956471/) and [lipid review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2692399/) support these distinctions.

**Area estimate — pass with truthful access limit.** Independently read [Helander & Fändriks 2014 PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24694282/). It supports the adult mucosal estimate, methods and substantial variability. The chapter explicitly says the full subscription paper was unavailable and does not claim 30 m² was measured in Kym. The subtraction is approximate; the old tennis-court estimate is rejected rather than silently repeated.

**Gallbladder case — pass.** Liver production continues; storage/concentration and the delivery pattern change. An open remaining duct route is an explicit premise.
