# One adventure: how the biology chapters are told (*The Living World*)

*The Living World* reads as one continuous forensic investigation: four hundred years of curious people asking what separates a living seed from a flickering flame, how a hundred-tonne banyan tree builds itself out of thin air, how a four-letter molecule stores the recipe for a brain, and how simple ocean cells diversified into every creature on Earth.

## The shape of every section

1. **A scene or a puzzle she can feel.** `:::scene Florence, summer 1668` or a moment from her own day in Mumbai: dry *moong* beans sprouting on the kitchen counter, the Western Ghats turning emerald within 48 hours of the first June monsoon downpour, mangroves standing in salty tidewater along Carter Road, or *idli* batter rising overnight.
2. **Her likely wrong idea, said out loud and taken seriously.** Most wrong ideas in biology were believed by brilliant people for centuries: that rotting meat breeds maggots by itself, that trees eat soil to grow heavy, that lungs are where respiration happens, or that organisms mutate *in order to* survive.
3. **Predict first** (`:::predict`). She commits to an answer before the evidence is revealed.
4. **The decisive evidence.** The historical experiment (`:::evidence`) that settled the question—and a version she can run in a Mumbai kitchen (`:::home`) or explore in an interactive 2D/3D model (`:::live`).
5. **The better model, explained the simplest true way** (`BOOKPLAN.md` & `PLAYBOOK.md` §9). Phenomenon first, name second (`==term==` paired with a `:::note`). Mechanism instead of teleological shorthand: never "the root wants water" or "the bacterium mutates to beat the drug".
6. **`:::five Term` ("Understand it" card)** right after each central concept is taught: 5 core questions (`What is it?`, `Why is it called this?`, `What problem did it solve?`, `Why does it work?`, `How does it help?`), 3 folded `??` questions (`How do we know?`, `What it isn't`, `Where does it stop working?`), and a one-sentence explain-back saved in her notebook.
7. **`:::careful`** with the tempting misconception in her own voice, and why it fails.
8. **`:::school`** bridging to how Cambridge IGCSE Biology (0610) / Lower Secondary / IB MYP words the same idea, warning her of classic mark-scheme traps.
9. **`:::check`**, one per section, with the reasoned answer at the back of the book.
